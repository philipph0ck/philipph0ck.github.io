Traveling

Notes and photos from the road. Some are short, some are long, none of them are guidebooks.

April 2026 · Lisbon, Portugal

Tiles, trams, and pastéis

Spent a long weekend wandering the hills of Alfama. The thing nobody tells you about Lisbon is that every street is either going up or down — there is no flat. By day three my calves were begging for mercy.

Highlight: an unplanned evening at a tiny fado bar in Mouraria. No tourists, no menu, just a woman singing as if her heart was breaking in real time.

February 2026 · Norwegian fjords

Three days without seeing the sun

Bergen in February is a study in different shades of gray. Rain, clouds, fog, more rain. And somehow, completely magical. Sat in a coffee shop watching the harbor disappear and reappear for two hours straight.

Took the train to Flåm. The descent through the mountains might be the most beautiful thing I've ever seen from a window seat.

October 2025 · Kyoto, Japan

The temple at 6 a.m.

Set an alarm for 5:30 to beat the tour buses to Fushimi Inari. Worth every yawn. Walked the first kilometer of torii gates completely alone, just me and the sound of crows. By 8 a.m. it looked like a music festival.
